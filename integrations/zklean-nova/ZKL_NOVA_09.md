## Boundaries

This is a fixed-capacity, restricted implicational calculus. Its type equality uses exact row IDs, and the proof-term language is much smaller than Lean. Deterministic salts and public witness fixtures are not hiding. A compressed proof is not by itself evidence of the required privacy policy. No Bitcoin verifier or universal settlement bound is demonstrated. The next gate after successful compression should extend the calculus with a checked term DAG and locally nameless substitution and conversion, using separate conformance and false-statement tests.
