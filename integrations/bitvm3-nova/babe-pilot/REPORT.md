## 1. Executive Summary of Architectural Findings

1. **Committed `M14` Baseline Verified**:
   Committed results in [`../m14/README.md`](../m14/README.md) and [`../m14/circuit/RESULTS.json`](../m14/circuit/RESULTS.json) (`transcript_c4_v2_real_verification = 11,198,078` gates; full wrap verifier = **`785,442,492` non-free gates** and **`12,567,079,872` bytes (`12.57 GB` / `11.70 GiB`)** of ciphertexts per circuit across `8,635,080` input bits) match the reference figures. Under malicious-garbler `(N = 115, M = 39)` Cut-and-Choose (`C&C`, $2^{-53}$ soundness), replicating monolithic `M14` would generate **`1.445 TB`** and retain **`490.12 GB`** of ciphertexts per claim, and worst-case on-chain input assertion would require **`17,990` Taproot inputs (`~208.5 MVBytes`)**.
2. **Transparent Zero-Knowledge Input-Label Soldering Works End-to-End on CPU**:
   `nova_snark::nova::CompressedSNARK` proves the hybrid **Base-SHA256 + Soldered-Poseidon** relation $\mathcal{R}_{\text{solder}}$ in true zero knowledge (`11,688`-byte proof, **`1.036 s`** verification time), closing the $1/N$ base-instance equivocation gap between Bitcoin Tapscript `OP_SHA256` hashlocks and algebraic Poseidon commitments while reducing on-chain active-label delivery by **$M\times$** (`3.0x` at pilot `M = 3`; `39.0x` at production `M = 39`).
3. **255-bit Pasta `w = 4` Windowed Witness-Assisted EC Step Beats Both `FC03` and `M14` Without Trusted Setup**:
   Our measured 255-bit Pallas/Vesta `w = 4`-bit windowed witness-assisted complete affine EC + SHA-256 step (`eval_pasta_window_ec_step`, `W = 3,832` input wires) compiles to **`769,351` non-free gates (`12,309,616` B = `11.74 MiB` ciphertext/circuit)** and garbles in **`0.559 s`** / evaluates in **`0.359 s`** on 2 CPU threads:
   - **`1,020.9x` fewer gates per circuit** than monolithic `M14` (`785.44M` gates);
   - **`9.47x` fewer gates per circuit** than `FC03`'s 1-bit projective RCB `BindingStep` (`7.29M` gates) **and `4.0x` fewer Pedersen trace steps** (`8,388,672` vs `33,554,688` steps for $n = 2^{17}$), yielding a **`37.9x` reduction** in total gate-step product;
   - **`1.89x` fewer gates per circuit** than Babylon's Groth16/BN254 garbled verifier (`1.45M` gates, `22.16 MiB`), while **eliminating Groth16's circuit-specific trusted setup**.
4. **Standard Single-Transaction Bitcoin Witness at Full 255-bit Pasta Scale**:
   Constructing the real multi-input Taproot `AssertLabelsTx` (`CheckLampSigsMatch` chunked at `480` wires/input across `8` UTXO inputs for `W = 3,832` wires) yields **`88,381 vB` (`353,522 WU < 400,000 WU`)** with `961 <= 1,000` stack items/input and `64 <= 80` bytes max item size—fitting inside **one standard Bitcoin Core 31.1 transaction**, while the worst-case `1-of-39` `DisproveTx` (`40`-leaf balanced Taptree, depth 6) measures **`190 vB` (`757 WU`)**.

## 6. Remaining Blockers Before Production Deployment

1. **Nova Soldering Proving Throughput & `VerifierKey` Footprint at `W = 3,832, M = 39`**:
   - At pilot scale (`W = 128, M = 3`, `122,856` R1CS constraints/step), CPU-only `RecursiveSNARK` + `CompressedSNARK` proving takes `189.1 s` on 2 threads (`~1.48 s`/wire) and produces a `50.43 MiB` reusable transparent IPA `VerifierKey` (`11.41 KiB` proof).
   - Scaling to `W = 3,832, M = 39` linearly (`2` SHA-256s + `76` Poseidons $\approx 77\text{k}$ constraints/wire) represents `~295M` total R1CS constraints (`~40–60 min` on a 16-core CPU using tree/parallel `HyperNova`/`SuperNova` or multi-segment Nova folding, with zero GPU required). Packing `8` wires/step with parallel segment folding is the natural optimization step.
2. **Bounded Fan-In R1CS Row Decomposition (`FC03` Integration)**:
   - While `eval_pasta_window_ec_step` reduces the Pedersen witness-binding trace step to `769,351` gates (`11.74 MiB`/circuit), `FC03`'s unsplit R1CS `Row` check still reaches `50.18M` gates (p99) and `1.15B` gates (max width) on wide linear combinations. Splitting wide rows into fixed fan-in (`k = 8` or `16` terms/step) authenticated partial-sum transitions brings every local dispute leaf under `~1M` non-free gates (`< 16 MiB`/circuit) and `< 4,096` input wires (single standard `AssertLabelsTx`).
3. **Pre-Signed Connector Graph & Watchtower Persistence**:
   - The pilot verifies standalone consensus-valid Taproot leaf scripts, control blocks, and standardness bounds (`bitcoin` v0.32). Integrating multi-input `AssertLabelsTx` connectors with pre-signed MuSig2 covenant sessions and hardened single-use disk journals (matching `m14-claim` PR #25) is required before mainnet/signet deployment.
