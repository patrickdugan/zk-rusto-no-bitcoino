# ZKL-17: capture-avoiding substitution and beta conversion

Original bounded raw lambda calculus: variable (de Bruijn index), inert constant, lambda, application. A public canonical, topologically ordered term arena is independently reconstructed by the verifier; capacity 64 nodes. Term IDs bind syntax through a domain-separated Poseidon root. Private certificate row payloads encode Lift(t,k,u), Sub(t,k,s,u), or Eq(t,u), authenticated against a second append-only Merkle tree (capacity 64). The verifier must construct the public arena, goal, initial empty log and zero cursor/last judgment and pin the circuit parameters. Public arena contents leak syntax; there is no witness privacy claim.

Lifting adds one to indices at or above a cutoff, increasing the cutoff beneath lambda. Substitution removes a binder: indices below k remain, index k is replaced with s, indices above k decrement. Beneath lambda the replacement is lifted at cutoff zero, then the body is substituted at k+1. Every recursive premise is a separately authenticated earlier row. Conversion consists of reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity, beta, and congruence under lambda/application. All 17 cases are selected by constrained one-hot bits; every case contributes the same fixed shape. The public arena uses structural canonicalization, so equality of term IDs is equality of syntax. Integer comparisons use 16-bit bounded differences. By induction on the backward references, the accepted rows express the described raw-term relations, conditional on the validated arena and hash binding. No arbitrary Lean typing judgment, universe, dependent Pi, eta, delta/iota or theoremhood claim. Untyped beta conversion is not a terminating normalization algorithm.
